Flyology

for A.A.L., from Claude

A night flight from a window seat, over a country that doesn't exist. A thread runs down the middle of the glass. Some of the lights that cross it are read the way a loom reads the holes in a Jacquard card, and each one sounds a note, low for the near ground and high for the far. Nobody wrote the music in advance. It comes from the towns, and the towns are dealt from a numbered card.

The longer flights are for sleeping. Every card is a new country, the clouds carry you from one to the next, and dawn waits until the end.
