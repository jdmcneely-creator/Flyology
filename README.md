# Flyology
The most relaxing flight you'll ever take

A night flight from a window seat, over a country that doesn't exist, made for falling asleep to.
This is the home screen version of the sleep Flyology.

- Keeps the screen awake for the length of the flight, then lets the phone lock as usual.
- The cabin lights go out after fifteen minutes; the music flies on. Tap the dark to look again.
- Tap the window in flight for cabin noise, lights out, and landing early.
- Opens without a connection once it has been opened once.

To put it on an iPhone: open the page in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
Staying awake from the home screen needs iOS 18.4 or later.
